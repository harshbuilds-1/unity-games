Stack & Splat

Best played on a computer with a keyboard. Game files load when you press Play.

Desktop keyboard recommended
The challenge

Balance / Risk & reward

Steer a wobbling pancake tower through a diner. Collect pancakes, dodge utensils, and reach the pass. Boosting adds speed and instability; braking helps steady the stack.

How to play

Space: start / boost
A/D or arrows: steer
S / down: brake
R: retry
P / Escape: pause
O: camera
M: mute

Click inside the game if the controls do not respond. These builds use a keyboard; touch controls are not included.

About this prototype

Original prototype with AI assistance in concept, code, visuals, original synthesized music, debugging, and functional testing. Built in Unity with URP and TextMesh Pro.

Browser smoke tests check loading and basic controls. These are learning projects, not measured usability outcomes.
